# *Aria Nostra* (Our Air) — Screenplay Project

A socially conscious dramatic feature exploring air quality, environmental justice (EJ), and public health disparities across three historic Italian-American urban enclaves: **Bensonhurst (Brooklyn, NY)**, the **North End (Boston, MA)**, and **South Philadelphia (Philadelphia, PA)**.

---

## Dramatic Premise

*Aria Nostra* weaves together the multi-generational struggles of working-class Italian-American families who have lived for generations alongside heavy infrastructure, freight corridors, and toxic industrial operations. Through intimate domestic drama and community activism, the story investigates the quiet crisis of toxic air, respiratory illness, and environmental neglect in historic neighborhoods often perceived purely through culinary nostalgia or mob tropes rather than the gritty, complex realities of environmental justice.

---

## Focal Neighborhoods & Environmental Profiles

### 1. Bensonhurst, Brooklyn, New York
* **Geographic & Infrastructural Context:** Flanked by the heavily trafficked Belt Parkway (Shore Parkway) and adjacent to the Gowanus Expressway (I-278) connector freight corridor. Traversed by arterial truck routes (86th Street, Bay Parkway, Stillwell Ave) and legacy multi-family heating infrastructure (#4 and #6 heavy residual oils).
* **Key Environmental Pollutants:** Nitrogen Dioxide ($\text{NO}_2$), fine particulate matter ($\text{PM}_{2.5}$), and black carbon (diesel soot).
* **Health Disparities:** Elevated rates of adult Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD), cardiovascular stress, and pediatric asthma emergency department visits; vulnerability among elderly residents who spend long hours outdoors on front porches and commercial avenues.

### 2. North End, Boston, Massachusetts
* **Geographic & Infrastructural Context:** Historically severed by the elevated Central Artery (I-93) and subsequent Big Dig construction; bounded by tunnel portals and massive ventilation buildings (Vent Building 7, Callahan/Sumner Tunnels) discharging concentrated underground exhaust; situated directly across Boston Harbor under primary Logan International Airport takeoff/landing flight corridors.
* **Key Environmental Pollutants:** Ultrafine particles ($\text{UFPs} < 0.1\,\mu\text{m}$) from aviation jet fuel exhaust, roadway $\text{PM}_{2.5}$, and marine diesel emissions from harbor vessels.
* **Health Disparities:** Ultrafine particles penetrate deep into alveolar lung tissue and vascular systems, linked to childhood asthma clusters, elevated cardiovascular events, and chronic respiratory inflammation in dense, poorly ventilated brick tenement buildings.

### 3. South Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
* **Geographic & Infrastructural Context:** Sits in the immediate shadow of the former Philadelphia Energy Solutions (PES) Refinery complex—which operated for nearly 150 years (1870–2019) along the lower Schuylkill River. Brackets by I-95, I-76 (Schuylkill Expressway), massive CSX/Norfolk Southern freight rail yards, and diesel traffic servicing the Packer Avenue Marine Terminal and Food Distribution Center.
* **Key Environmental Pollutants:** Volatile organic compounds including carcinogenic benzene plumes, sulfur dioxide ($\text{SO}_2$), hydrogen fluoride (legacy explosion risk), and coarse/fine particulates ($\text{PM}_{10}, \text{PM}_{2.5}$).
* **Health Disparities:** Known as one of the most polluted urban zip codes in Pennsylvania with some of the highest pediatric asthma hospitalization rates in the state, severe adult asthma, and multi-generational cancer clusters across working-class Italian-American and Black neighborhoods.
